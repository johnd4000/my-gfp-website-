Financial Planning

Strategies to manage your money and reach your life goals, built around the stage of life you're in.

What it covers

Financial planning, explained

Financial planning is about building strategies to manage your financial affairs and meet your life goals. The first step is getting the right advice, and working with a professional planner gives you confidence that your future is on track.

We can help with budgeting and cash flow, savings plans, superannuation, tax planning, home loan repayments, debt reduction, insurance, investments and retirement.

Every stage of life

A plan for where you are now

Your goals change as your life does, so your plan should too. Here's what tends to matter at each stage.

Young to mid-life

Building your career and perhaps starting a family.

  • Buying your first home
  • Travelling
  • Paying off study debt
  • Getting married
  • Having children
  • Family healthcare
  • Business planning

Mid-life

Consolidating: a comfortable lifestyle and your long-term future.

  • Investments
  • Home renovations
  • Tax and debt management
  • Inheritance
  • Retirement planning
  • Income protection
  • Long-term care planning
  • Healthcare

Pre-retirement

With 20 or more years of retirement ahead, preparation matters.

  • Eliminating debt
  • Helping your children
  • Retirement planning
  • Wills and trusts
  • Business exit strategy
  • Family healthcare

Retirement

Time for hobbies, travel and family, and for passing on your wealth.

  • Protecting your assets
  • Preserving your capital
  • Travel
  • Aged care planning
  • Gifting to family
  • Estate planning
  • Healthcare
