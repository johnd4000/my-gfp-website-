About Partners in Planning

Financial advice tailored to your values

We are a Melbourne-based financial planning practice working with professionals, families and business owners who want clarity about their future, and who care where their money is invested along the way.

Our process starts with you. We take the time to understand what matters to you most, then build a financial strategy around your values, one that moves you confidently toward financial security.

Why Choose Us?

We firmly believe in our motto ‘Not all financial planners are built the same’.

We believe in the importance of unbiased financial advice. To ensure that advice is truly in your best interests, it must be free from conflicts of interest.

Many financial planning firms claim to be ‘privately owned or self-licensed,’ but that doesn’t necessarily mean they offer genuinely independent advice.

At Partners in Planning, we proudly maintain our 100% true independence — we do not accept commissions on any financial products, including insurance. Our only compensation comes directly from you, our clients. This ensures that we work solely in your best interest.

The green in our name

Values-aligned investing, without the guesswork

Sustainable investing has a marketing problem. Plenty of funds carry the label; far fewer can show you what they actually hold.

We look past the badge to the underlying holdings, screening methodology and disclosure quality, so the portfolio you end up with genuinely matches the values you told us mattered. Where a sustainable option would compromise your objectives, we will tell you that too.
