How we help

You will always know the next step

Our process is deliberately unhurried. You will never be asked to decide on something you have not had time to read and understand.

Who we work with

Advice shaped to where you are

Building wealth

Professionals and couples in their 30s and 40s balancing a mortgage, growing super and the first serious investment decisions.

Approaching retirement

Those within ten years of stopping work who need to know whether the numbers actually add up — and what to change if they do not.

In retirement

Retirees managing drawdown, Centrelink interaction and estate considerations while keeping the portfolio resilient.

Business owners

Owners coordinating personal wealth, business structure, succession and the insurance that protects both.

Our process

Five stages, no surprises

01

Introduction — at our cost

Around an hour, in person or online. We listen to what you are trying to achieve and explain how we work, what we charge and what we would need from you. You leave with a clear sense of whether to continue.

02

Discovery

We gather the full picture: income, expenses, assets, debts, superannuation, existing insurance and estate arrangements — plus the values you want your investments to reflect.

03

Strategy & written advice

You receive a Statement of Advice setting out our recommendations, the reasoning behind each, the risks, the alternatives we considered and the total cost. We walk you through it together before anything proceeds.

04

Implementation

We handle the paperwork, applications and rollovers, and keep you updated at each stage so nothing lands unexpectedly.

05

Ongoing review

Scheduled reviews keep the strategy current as legislation, markets and your own circumstances shift. You can reach us between reviews whenever something changes.
