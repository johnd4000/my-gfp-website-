Every part of the plan, working together

Most people arrive with one question. It usually turns out to be connected to four others, so we look at the whole picture.

01 · Retirement and Super

Knowing whether the numbers work

Before you start planning your retirement, there are some key factors to consider. We model what it will actually cost, test that against different market and longevity scenarios, and identify the levers that make the biggest difference: contribution levels, retirement age, drawdown rate and asset allocation.

On the superannuation side, that means consolidating lost or duplicated accounts, reviewing the fees and insurance held inside your fund, and setting a contribution strategy that uses the concessional and non-concessional caps sensibly.

We compare the investment options within your fund, including sustainable options, on what they actually hold rather than what they are called. Where Age Pension entitlements are relevant, we factor them in properly rather than treating them as an afterthought.

02 · Financial Planning

A plan shaped around your goals

The starting point to great financial advice is catering to your specific needs and goals. Before recommending anything, we map where you are now (income, expenses, assets, debts and the obligations you carry) and what you are actually working toward.

From there we set the structure: how your cashflow is organised, which goals come first, what each one realistically requires, and how the pieces fit together rather than competing with one another.

You receive it in writing, in plain English, with the reasoning behind every recommendation and what it costs. It is a plan you can follow, not a document you file away.

03 · Investing

Portfolios built for your timeframe

For you and many other Australians, a secure financial future is a top priority. We build diversified portfolios around your objectives, your timeframe and your genuine tolerance for volatility, then rebalance them with discipline rather than reaction.

We are transparent about costs at every layer, because fees compound against you exactly as returns compound for you.
